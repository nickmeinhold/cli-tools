# CRUCIBLE — scout as an opportunity engine (offense)

*The enthusiasm case, carried forward for the adversary to strike. Heat and
evidence are kept separate on purpose.*

## The pick

Flip the shipped "watchman" surprise engine (`~/git/tools/cli-tools/scout` — live,
merged, 16 tests: persisted Bayesian belief, nightly loop, KL(posterior‖prior)
surprise, weighted-log-sum ranking, Telegram-at-dawn) from **defense to offense**.
Same Kalman heart (carry belief → observe → rank divergence), opposite direction:
a divergence stops being an *alarm* and becomes an *opening*. It doesn't tell Nick
what broke; it tells him what just became **possible**.

Naming resolution: the defensive watchman gets renamed (→ **Parallax**); **scout**
is freed for this offense engine (a scout ranges ahead and finds things to *do*).

## Five opportunity axes (Nick greenlit all five)

1. **Convergence** — two of your own repos drifting *toward* each other (shared
   code/concept growth across a repo pair; the merge you sketched is now N files apart).
2. **Unblocking** — a task you shelved whose blocker just cleared (cross-ref open/
   shelved tasks against what shipped in the fleet).
3. **Latent abstraction** — you've built the same thing N times; it wants to be a
   library (duplicated code/pattern across repos).
4. **Adjacency** — a person/repo/tool that rhymes with your work just appeared
   (ties Task #4 social-harvester; consent-gated — leads only, never auto-contact).
5. **Recombination** — two of your capabilities that are secretly one machine
   (`/recombine` + curvature-engine, made continuous).

## Why it glows (heat — NOT evidence)

A machine for *"oh, I could do that now,"* every morning, over a sprawl too large
to hold in one head. The most alive tooling surfaces possibility, not failure.

## The falsifier (what would prove this is slag)

**Signal-to-noise.** An opportunity engine whose axes mostly surface noise or the
already-known is a nag, not a scout. Two repos sharing "graph" ≠ convergence; a
helper duplicated 3× may *want* to stay duplicated; a "cleared blocker" may be one
Nick already acted on. **If a hand-run of the convergence + latent-abstraction axes
over the real fleet yields mostly obvious-or-junk, the ore is slag.** The engine
lives or dies on precision, not on the elegance of the five-axis framing. Heat must
find how serendipity/recommender systems fight exactly this; Cast must state the
per-axis precision mechanism, not assert it.

## Claims to falsify (for Temper)

- That "belief-shift surprise" is even the right ranking for *opportunities* (a
  surprising convergence may be less actionable than an unsurprising-but-valuable one —
  surprise ≠ value; the watchman conflated them and got away with it because problems
  are urgent; openings are not).
- That five axes are separable and not really one (recombination ⊇ convergence?).
- That the data sources (git history, task list, memory graph) actually contain the
  signal each axis needs, at usable density.
- That an opportunity a machine surfaces is one Nick couldn't more cheaply surface himself.
